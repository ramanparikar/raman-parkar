Gaurabda year & masa

Selected date · year from published transition dates Loading…

SCSMath marks Gaurabda year transitions at Gaura Purnima: year 541 began on 4 March 2026, and year 542 began on 23 March 2027. Its ICS files list observances rather than a tithi and masa value for every Gregorian date, so this page does not infer daily tithi, paksa, or nakshatra.

Masa sequence in this cycle: Madhava, Govinda, Vishnu, Madhusudan, Trivikram (first half), Purushottam (extra month, 17 May–15 June 2026), Trivikram (second half), Vaman, Sridhar, Hrishikesh, Padmanabha, Damodar, Keshava, Narayana.
