# BBT rights and permissions checklist

This checklist is for the proposed searchable scripture knowledge base. It is not a permission grant.

## Official guidance reviewed
The Bhaktivedanta Book Trust's permissions page says requests must be in writing and should identify:
- the title(s);
- whether requested material is text or illustrations;
- relevant page numbers;
- the project and proposed use.

The BBT page describes a general permissions turnaround of 4–8 weeks. It also distinguishes incidental use for ISKCON promotional material from projects consisting mostly or entirely of BBT material. A comprehensive searchable scripture database is not to be assumed covered by incidental-use guidance.

Official BBTI permissions page: https://www.bbt.org/permissions

## Verify the authorised rights contact before relying on a licence

As of the research date (1 November 2026), a separate site at https://officialbbt.org/
publishes a notice asserting that the 1972 Bhaktivedanta Book Trust owns the copyrights
and is regularising its trusteeship and licensing arrangements. The notice asks parties
to confirm arrangements with that Trust rather than BBT International and itself says
its trusteeship is still being confirmed by a California court. This is a material
rights-chain issue, not something this project can resolve.

Accordingly, do not assume that a response from any single website or organisation
alone is sufficient. Before importing BBT editorial material, obtain legal advice and
written confirmation from the party or parties with demonstrable authority for the
specific edition and territory. Verify the chain of title, current trusteeship/agency,
and any existing licence authority. Keep all such material out of the database until
this is resolved.

Other official-facing references to review:
- BBT book catalog: https://www.bbt.org/book/en-bg
- Separate Trust notice: https://officialbbt.org/
- Separate Trust original-books catalogue: https://officialbbt.org/original-books

## Scope to request explicitly
Ask for written confirmation covering:
- storage and reproduction of the selected edition's Sanskrit text, transliteration, translations, word meanings, purports/commentaries, introductions, indexes, and editorial apparatus;
- public display on ramanparikar.com and any mobile application;
- full-text search indexing and snippets;
- embeddings/vector indexing, semantic search, and AI retrieval/generation;
- caching, backups, exports, and administrator access;
- languages, territories, domains, and duration;
- whether the grant covers the exact edition/printing and any future revised editions;
- required copyright notices, attribution, restrictions, fees, and expiry/termination;
- whether any materials may be redistributed or made downloadable.

## Internal controls until written permission is received
- Do not scrape or bulk-copy Vedabase, BBT, or third-party digital text.
- Do not import protected translations, word meanings, purports, scans, or artwork.
- Keep rights state as `unknown` or `permission_pending`.
- Keep all content private and excluded from public search and AI retrieval.
- Record the permission document as a source and map each permission to the specific edition and permitted actions.
- Re-check grant expiry and permitted actions at request time, not only during import.

This checklist does not determine whether any particular Sanskrit text or translation is public domain in every territory. Obtain rights advice where needed.
