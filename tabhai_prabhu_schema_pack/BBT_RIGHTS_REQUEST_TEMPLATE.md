# Draft rights-clearance request: scripture knowledge base

**To:** The verified copyright owner and authorised licensing representative(s) for the specific editions listed below

**Subject:** Written permission request for a non-commercial Gaudiya Vaishnava scripture study and search project

Dear Rights and Permissions Team,

Raman Parikar is developing a non-commercial educational scripture knowledge base for students and devotees. The proposed project will organise canonical references and, only where expressly authorised, selected edition text and associated editorial material for study and search.

We are writing before importing or publishing any protected material. We would appreciate confirmation of the current copyright ownership, licensing authority and the correct party or parties authorised to grant the permissions described below for each specific edition.

**Works and editions under consideration**
- *Bhagavad-gita As It Is*: the 1972 edition and later BBT catalogue formats, each treated as a distinct edition until verified.
- *Srimad-Bhagavatam*: the original canto publications and the later 12-volume / 18-volume collections, with volume and edition mapping kept separate.
- *Sri Caitanya-caritamrita*: the original publication series and the later 9-volume / one-volume formats, kept separate.

**Requested written permission (only if you are authorised to grant it)**

Please indicate separately whether permission is granted for each of the following:
1. Digital storage and limited reproduction of the Sanskrit/Bengali source text and any authorised transliteration.
2. Digital storage and reproduction of English or other translations.
3. Word-for-word meanings, synonyms, purports, commentaries, introductions, summaries, indexes, illustrations and other editorial matter.
4. Public display on the Raman Parikar website and any future mobile application.
5. Full-text search indexing, excerpts/snippets and internal search indexes.
6. Embeddings, semantic/vector search, and retrieval-augmented AI responses.
7. Use of the material in AI prompts or model context, including whether generated answers may quote or paraphrase it.
8. Caching, backups, administrator access and disaster recovery.
9. Download, export, redistribution or sharing with third parties (if any).

Please specify the exact editions/printings, languages, territory, domains, permitted user groups, term, attribution and copyright notices, any fees or restrictions, and the process for renewal, amendment, expiry or withdrawal. We will not assume that permission for one edition or format covers another.

**Authority and evidence**

Because public notices currently refer to different BBT entities and to ongoing trusteeship/licensing regularisation, please also provide:
- the legal name of the copyright owner for each work/edition;
- the legal basis and documentary evidence of the signatory's authority to license the requested uses;
- confirmation whether any other entity's consent is required;
- the current status of any prior agency, licence or distribution arrangement relevant to these editions.

We understand that a catalogue listing, a public website, or permission to sell a printed book does not by itself grant the digital, indexing or AI permissions requested here.

Until we receive and verify written permission, we will not copy, scrape, upload, publicly display, index or use protected text or editorial material for AI retrieval. The database will retain only bibliographic metadata and independently verified canonical-reference records.

Thank you for your guidance. We would be glad to provide a technical description of the project and its safeguards.
