About us

About Raman Parikar

A spiritual community rooted in the Radharaman Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition and dedicated to the study and sharing of devotional wisdom.

Who we are

A community of learning and devotion

Raman Parikar is a spiritual organisation based in Nangloi–Kirari, Delhi. The community is devoted to spreading the teachings of Gaudiya Shastra, Bhagavad Gita, Srimad Bhagavatam and other sacred texts.

Our approach is centered on sincere study, devotional practice, seva and spiritual association, with Shri Radharaman Ji as a central devotional focus.

Our guiding principle

We seek to make scriptural wisdom accessible while encouraging sincere reflection, devotional practice and meaningful spiritual community.

Tradition

Our devotional lineage

Raman Parikar describes itself as coming within the Radharaman Gaudiya Vaishnava parampara, with inspiration from the teachings of Shri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and Shri Gopal Bhatta Goswami.

Shri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu

The community looks to the teachings and devotional example of Shri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu as a foundational inspiration.

Shri Gopal Bhatta Goswami

The Gaudiya tradition and its rich body of devotional literature form an important part of Raman Parikar's spiritual orientation.

Present guidance

Raman Parikar is guided by Shri Pundrik Goswami Ji and Smt. Renuka Goswami Ji.

Our home

Nangloi–Kirari, Delhi

Raman Parikar is based in Delhi and seeks to build a wider spiritual community through study, satsanga, seva and online outreach.
